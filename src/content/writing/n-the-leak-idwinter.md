𝑰𝒏 𝒕𝒉𝒆 𝑩𝒍𝒆𝒂𝒌 𝑴𝒊𝒅𝒘𝒊𝒏𝒕𝒆𝒓

You should shut that door.
 Can't—it was blown broken!
You always build new ones.
 A bleak one, you say!
 I'll save an empire instead.
Nothing can shut it now?
 A merciful named bullet will.
