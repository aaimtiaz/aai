পাহাড়ে যাতি হবে, বৃষ্টি দেখার জন্যি...!

Rain has a few interesting powers: it gently carries us into soft flashbacks and stirs up stormy feelings. Its rhythm awakens a sense of comfort, wrapping the heart in memories that feel both distant and vividly near.

In the hush of rain, through a pane blurred,
Thoughts remain—soft and unheard.
That even in crowds, a heart can still yearn
For just one light, the storm longs to return.
