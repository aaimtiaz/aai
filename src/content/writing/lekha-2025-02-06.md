মানুষের প্রধান কাজ মায়া তৈরি করা, এবং মায়া কাটানো।

292 days...!!! An unforgettable journey at Reaz Public School! From the very first day, the stunning architecture captivated me. But as days passed, I realized that the true beauty lay in the lives around me—my students and colleagues, who became a cherished part of my heart.

Now, as I say goodbye, I see the tears in my students' eyes, and it breaks my heart to have put them in this situation. I feel terrible, yet deeply honored by their love. Though I must leave, the memories and emotions will stay forever.
