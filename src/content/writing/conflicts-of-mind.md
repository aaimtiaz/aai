Conflicts of mind:🫣

"Shayad woh bhi accha hota
Agar chor mera samaan le jata.
Aur dekho, yeh bhi keh rahe hain hum
Samaan kas ke bandh kar."

"In Pachidgi's shadow, treasures I bind,
Yet, steal my essence and let my belongings unwind."

These lines convey a poetic expression of inner conflict and regret. "In Pachidgi's shadow" refers to the presence of regret or remorse. "Treasures I bind" indicates the speaker is safeguarding valuable things. However, the lines also express a paradoxical desire for a release from this burden, suggesting that despite securing belongings, the speaker is willing to let them go for a sense of liberation ("let my belongings unwind").
