As winter is fading, feel the moment—music might help a bit. 😄

A car ride through a foggy morning in winter,

where you stare at the rising sun from the rear corner.

While you try to keep yourself warmer and warmer,

the freezing wind numbs your nose and ears

and fills your eyes with tears.

Still, you can’t help but stare...!
