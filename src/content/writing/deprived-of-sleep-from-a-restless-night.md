Deprived of sleep from a restless night,
A day of travel, wearied in its flight,
At 21:30 past, I succumbed to dreams,
Yet midnight’s chime disrupted my serene.

With heavy eyes in the gentle rain,
I gaze upon the countryside again,
Through a misted window, blurred and gray,
I watch the night’s beauty softly sway.

"Thinking is more interesting than knowing
But less interesting than looking." – Johann Wolfgang
