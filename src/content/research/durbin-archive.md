Durbin is CASSA's astrophotography platform. Its previous website held a substantial collection of images and metadata with no straightforward way to move them.

I wrote a Python scraper to extract the images and their metadata from the old site into a central database, then helped coordinate a team of volunteers through a three-day sprint to clean the data and prepare **85 images** for the new platform. That produced 85 individual object pages and around **65 object essays**.

I also reviewed and finalised material before publication — two *Durer Kotha* articles and three object essays — and coordinated their release on the CASSA website and social channels.

It is not observational research, but it is the part of the work that decides whether anybody outside the field ever sees any of it.
