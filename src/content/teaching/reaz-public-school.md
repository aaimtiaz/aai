I taught science at Reaz Public School for 292 days, and was a member of the Corporate Social Responsibility team at Epyllion Group, Dhaka.

The part I would keep is the **Star Gazing Night** we ran at the school in January 2025 — telescopes on the school grounds, and a lot of students looking through an eyepiece for the first time. School science is mostly taught as a set of facts to be returned on an exam paper; a night like that is the cheapest available correction to that idea.
