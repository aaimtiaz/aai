I taught three junior undergraduate courses in the Department of Computer Science and Engineering:

- **PHY101** — mechanics, waves and oscillations, thermodynamics, optics
- **PHY102** — electricity and magnetism, modern physics
- **PHY103** — the accompanying laboratory

Teaching physics to computing students is a specific problem: the material has to earn its place against a curriculum they find more obviously relevant. Most of the work went into finding the examples that made a topic feel necessary rather than compulsory.
