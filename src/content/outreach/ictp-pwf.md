ICTP's Physics Without Frontiers runs research internships for students in countries where the local research infrastructure cannot yet support them. Mine was where the Sparkler galaxy project started, under Dr. Lamiya Mowla — work I have carried on since as a Graduate Research Assistant.

It is the clearest example I have of why programmes like this matter: without it, the project I now spend most of my time on would not exist.
