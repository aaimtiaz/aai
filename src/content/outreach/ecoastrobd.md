EcoAstroBD builds affordable, eco-friendly astronomy kits for schools that will never have an observatory budget. I advise on leadership, coordination and the overall direction of the platform.

The project is funded by a grant from the **IAU Office of Astronomy for Development** and launched across three districts, including Brahmanbaria. Alongside the kits it covers hands-on learning and teacher capacity building — the part that decides whether equipment gets used after the launch event.
