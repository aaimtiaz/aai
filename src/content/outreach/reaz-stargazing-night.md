I was teaching science at Reaz Public School at the time, so this one was close to home: telescopes set up on the school grounds and an evening of students queuing at the eyepiece.

There is a specific moment when someone looks through a telescope and realises the thing they are seeing is real and is out there right now. It is not replaceable by a photograph, and it is the entire argument for dragging equipment out to a school field.
