I joined CAM-SUST as Study Circle Secretary in 2019 and served as President of the 9th Executive Committee from March 2021 to July 2022.

What that actually involved:

- **Study circles** on astronomical image reduction, stars, celestial coordinate systems and exoplanets — including a session on radio astronomy I ran at the SUST University Center.
- **General meetings and stargazing nights**, which were the recruitment engine for everything else.
- **CAM-Talks** with invited speakers, including Dr. Khan M. B. Asad on detecting life on remote planets, and sessions on research ethics.
- **COSMANIA**, a science roadshow taken out to colleges — Sunamganj Government College among them.
- **নভোখেয়া**, the society's first online astronomy magazine, launched in 2022.
- Organising for the **University Physics Competition** and **Road to IAAC**, cross-posted across eight astronomy communities in Bangladesh.

I handed over to the next committee in August 2022.
