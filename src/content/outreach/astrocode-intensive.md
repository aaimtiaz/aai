A two-day workshop at SUST. I ran the second day, on astronomical image reduction — taking students through what actually has to happen to raw frames before anyone can make a measurement from them.

Most astronomy teaching stops at the pretty picture. The gap between that and a calibrated frame is where the subject actually lives, and it is very rarely taught to undergraduates.
