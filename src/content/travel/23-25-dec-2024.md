23-25 Dec 2024

~330 km of cycling in 3 days...!!! 🚴‍♂️

Max in a day: ~154 km

Route: Panchagarh → Nilphamari → Lalmonirhat → Kurigram → Jamalpur → Sherpur

Elimination is tough, but trips like this teach us how to carry only what we absolutely need. Huge thanks to my partners-in-pain for dragging me along the whole way—even though I made them wait at literally every stop, from the start to every single break. 😂

On this trip, we really got to soak in the vibe of northern Bangladesh—their everyday life, lively markets, chilly winter mornings, and even how the food tastes kept changing as we went from Panchagarh to Sherpur. Riding in the foggy morning was a whole experience on its own—some markets were still asleep, while others were just waking up. We could feel the freshness in the air from the smell of all kinds of vegetables—it was like the start of a new day coming alive. Oh, and the people? Their curiosity was something else—it felt awesome how interested and welcoming they were!

And finally, the ride through the Gojni jungle? Absolutely the best part! What an adventure! 🛤️
